## President

- Assign non-President officer roles
- Manage academic years, semesters and subjects

- Manage contributions and payment statuses
- Override groups
- Export authorized reports
- View full student numbers
- View audit logs and internal analytics
- Revoke sessions

## Vice President

The Vice President is the operational backup administrator.

The Vice President receives the President’s operational permissions except:

- Cannot assign or remove the President
- Cannot transfer President ownership

## Secretary

The Secretary can:

- Manage tasks
- Manage attendance
- Process attendance corrections
- Manage academic years, semesters and subjects
- Correct subject-group membership

The Secretary cannot:

- Publish official announcements
- Manage accounts

## Treasurer

The Treasurer can:

- Create contribution posts

- View contribution rosters
- Export contribution reports
- Send contribution reminders

The Treasurer cannot:

- Manage attendance
- Manage student accounts
- Publish general official announcements
- Assign officer roles
- View complete student numbers

- View announcements

- Mention students and groups

# Authentication design

Students log in with:

- Username
- Password

Accounts are created through:

- Excel/CSV import
- Manual administrator entry

Required student fields:

- Full name
- Username
- Student number

There are no profile pictures.

Use initials avatars.

Initial password:

- The student number

Changing the temporary password:

- Recommended
- Prompted during onboarding
- Not mandatory

Password resets:

- President or Vice President resets the password to the student number
- Audit every reset

Failed-login protection:

- Lock after five failed attempts
- Lockout duration: 15 minutes
- Clear the count after successful login

Sessions:

- Stay signed in
- Multiple devices allowed
- Users can review and terminate sessions
- Password reset, account deactivation or administrative revocation invalidates sessions

Student-number privacy:

- Student sees their own full number
- President and Vice President see full numbers
- Other users see masked numbers

Supabase Auth normally uses email or phone credentials. The visible login must remain username-based.

Use a documented internal identity strategy, such as a deterministic internal email derived from normalized username, while ensuring:

- Students never need to know the internal email
- Usernames remain unique
- Username normalization is consistent
- No real email verification is required
- Administrative user creation happens securely
- Service-role credentials never reach the frontend

If the chosen username strategy has a security problem, document and implement the safest compatible approach without changing the visible username login requirement.
